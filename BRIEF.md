## 4. DESIGN & TECHNICAL NOTES

**Design:**
- Clean, modern, professional. Not flashy.
- Mobile-first — most Kenyan users will view on mobile.
- Fast loading — optimise images, minimal animations. Kenyan internet speeds vary.
- WhatsApp click-to-chat floating button on every page (bottom right).
- Use real photos where possible, stock photos where needed.

**Technical:**
- Responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- SSL certificate (HTTPS)
- Fast hosting (optimise for East Africa)
- SEO basics: meta titles, descriptions, alt tags on images, sitemap
- Schema markup on every page (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ — practice what we preach)
- Google Analytics and/or simple analytics tracking
- Cookie consent if needed

**Integrations:**
- WhatsApp Business click-to-chat
- Calendly or booking tool embed
- Contact form (sends to email or Google Sheets)
- Google Maps embed on contact page
